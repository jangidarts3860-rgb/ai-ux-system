# Brand Patterns & Lessons

This file extracts the underlying lessons, trade-offs, and anti-patterns from major brand design systems. Use these as structural inspiration, not as strict rules to copy exactly.

## 1. The Power of a Unified Accent
- **Lesson**: Many premium brands use a single controlled accent color for interactive elements.
- **Trade-off**: Requires discipline.
- **What Not to Copy**: Do not copy the exact hue or brand palette.

## 2. Typographic Scales and Negative Tracking
- **Lesson**: Premium systems often use optical sizing and negative letter-spacing.
- **What Not to Copy**: Do not blindly apply values from a specific typeface.

## 3. Depth and Shadows
- **Lesson**: Multi-layered shadows create subtle realism.
- **What Not to Copy**: Do not copy a shadow stack without checking background and contrast.

## 4. Geometry and Radius
- **Lesson**: Corner radius sets the mood of a system.
- **Trade-off**: Sharp corners feel precise, round corners feel warm.

## 5. Information Density
- **Lesson**: Dense systems use deliberate hierarchy to fit more information.
- **Trade-off**: More density reduces breathing room.

---

## Final Rule

Learn the system, not the clone.
