# Brand Pattern: Clay

## Lessons & Core Philosophy
Clay blends warm cream surfaces, playful color, and quirky typography. It treats B2B data enrichment like a craft rather than a grunt task.

### Key Characteristics
- Warm cream canvas
- Playful accents and motion
- Distinctive handcrafted personality
- Strong hover micro-interaction system

## Final Rule
Take the emotion and interaction logic, not the exact identity.
